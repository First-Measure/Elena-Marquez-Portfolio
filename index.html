04 / Working process

Carry the idea through.

A project moves through repeated acts of reading, testing, and coordination. The sequence is iterative rather than strictly linear.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Read context, constraints, program, and existing conditions.

  2. 02

    Expose

    Make relationships and unresolved questions visible.

  3. 03

    Test

    Compare options through drawings, models, and focused studies.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Connect architectural intent with interdisciplinary information.

  5. 05

    Carry forward

    Preserve the governing idea as resolution increases.
